Who We Are

Unique by name, by finish

Unique Decorative Metals is a Coimbatore-based trading and supply house for decorative interior metal solutions, established in 2026.

We work with interior designers, architects, contractors and dealers across India, helping them source premium metal finishes without the usual hassle of searching, comparing and waiting.

From PVD and pad coated sheets to mirror frames and accessories, we focus on the details that make a space feel finished.
